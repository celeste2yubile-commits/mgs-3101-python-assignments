Coffee Shop Sales Analysis - Astoria

This analysis focused on the Astoria location.

Key Findings:
- 53,703 drinks were sold.
- 7,496 pastries were sold.
- Drink revenue was $157,553.96.
- Pastry revenue was $26,578.71.
- Total revenue was $184,132.67.

Recommendation 1:
Continue promoting coffee and tea because drinks generate most of the revenue.

Recommendation 2:
Offer drink-and-pastry bundle promotions to increase pastry sales.

Recommendation 3:
Focus marketing efforts on top-selling beverages because they drive the majority of sales.

Questions:
1. Should drinking chocolate be classified as a drink?
2. Should all bakery items be counted as pastries?
3. Would comparing all three locations provide better recommendations?
